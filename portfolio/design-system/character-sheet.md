# Character Design Sheet — Anastasia

## Identity source
Use only the portrait photographs supplied by the site owner as face references. The approved generated cover illustration is the first visual anchor for the magazine. Never use the RONDO model's illustrated woman as the identity source.

## Stable recognizable features
- Light skin tone.
- Blue-grey eyes with natural almond shape and spacing.
- Full, softly straight natural eyebrows.
- Natural nose bridge and tip.
- Natural lip contour and volume.
- Soft oval / gently angular facial outline, jaw and chin.
- Long light-brown hair with warm natural highlights.

## Illustration translation
Preserve the facial geometry while translating photo information into the RONDO-inspired editorial idiom: clear eye planes, controlled lashes, softly simplified nose, defined but delicate lips, warm cheek tint, intentional hair locks and painterly shape transitions. Stylization must not change age, eye spacing, nose width, jawline or hair color.

## Allowed variations
Outfit, pose, hand gesture, hair movement, accessories, camera angle, activity and scene can change. The face and stable hair identity remain consistent.

## Character wardrobe family
- Olive tailored suit with sculptural sleeves and an ivory bow.
- Cream blouse, dark skirt or tailored trousers.
- Dark gloves and restrained antique-gold jewellery.
- Practical editorial work look with a laptop, notebook or technical drawing.

## Expression / pose library
1. Composed cover portrait.
2. Reviewing an interface on a laptop.
3. Holding a small technical notebook / architecture diagram.
4. Writing and annotating project notes.
5. Calm direct portrait for contact page.

## Quality gate
Reject an illustration if the eyes become oversized, the nose is miniaturized, the chin sharply narrowed, lips inflated, hair becomes copper/red/short, or the face reads as a generic fashion model. The character is recognizable first; costume ornament is secondary.
